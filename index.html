The Story

Mötley Crüe Inc. is a World of Warcraft guild that goes back to the Ventrilo days, long before Discord. The Discord server came in June 2022, but the people had already been together for years. We showed up for the raids and stayed for each other: the music in Crüe FM, the jokes, the Rage Room, and the real-life channel.

The game ends for us here. The people who made it what it was are why this page exists.
