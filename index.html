The Story

Azeroth is big, but a good guild makes it feel small. Mötley Crüe Inc. was a World of Warcraft guild built on loud voice chat, loose raid rules and loyalty that outlasted every patch, expansion and wipe.

This page is for everyone who ever wore the tag: the pulls, the wipes, the loot drama, the late-night runs and the inside jokes nobody outside the guild will ever understand.
